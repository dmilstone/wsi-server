WSI Viewer Quick Guide

Header toolbar, channels, annotations, and export

1. Header and toolbar

  • All viewer tools are icon buttons in one compact toolbar row (hover or focus for names).
  • Help (?), the rightmost icon, opens this guide.

2. Open and navigate

  • Choose an image in Sample images on the left.
  • Use the Images toolbar icon to show or hide the image browser (there is no separate Open button).
  • Drag the slide to pan; scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Use Zoom in / Zoom out or Home to reset the initial home zoom.
  • Collapse side panels when you need more viewing space.

3. Settings and channels

  • Use the Settings toolbar icon to show or hide display controls on the right.
  • Use checkboxes to show or hide channels; choose LUT color and adjust opacity.
  • Use Reset display or Recompute auto in the Channels panel.

4. Create and select annotations

  • Click Rectangle to draw a rectangular annotation; click again to stop drawing.
  • Click an annotation directly to select it.
  • Press Delete or Backspace to remove the selected annotation.
  • Annotations are saved per image and persist when you switch images and return.

5. Annotation names and visibility

  • Use Annotations and Names in the toolbar to show or hide geometry and on-slide labels.
  • With one annotation selected, click its on-slide name label to edit inline.
  • Enter or leave the name field to commit; Escape cancels a draft.

6. Protect recent annotation work

  • Saving is asynchronous and currently has no visible Saved indicator.
  • After creating, moving, renaming, or deleting, pause several seconds before switching images, refreshing, or closing.
  • For important work, return to the image to confirm persistence.

7. Export regions

  • Export visible region exports the area currently visible at native resolution.
  • Export selected annotation exports the selected rectangle (select one annotation first).
  • Filenames use the slide name plus -region.png or -annotation-name.png when named.
  • Native exports are limited to 16,000,000 pixels. If too large, a dialog asks you to zoom in or choose a smaller region.
  • Your browser may ask where to save the PNG. Confirm the file opens.

8. Slide overview, full screen, presentation

  • Slide overview shows the slide label and whole-slide thumbnail when available.
  • Full screen uses the browser full-screen view.
  • Presentation hides the header, toolbar, side panels, and status bar for a larger slide view. Click Exit presentation or press Escape to leave.
  • Presentation mode does not yet provide a dedicated clean-view layout (future roadmap).

9. Environment and safety

  • Use only images appropriate for the assigned environment.
  • Never copy sensitive production material into development or staging.

10. Pilot feedback

  • Use Pilot Feedback in the Channels panel secondary links, or press F in the viewer, to open the in-view feedback panel without leaving your current slide.
  • The full-page form is at /pilot-feedback; results are at /pilot-feedback/results for authenticated users.
  • Do not enter patient identifiers, PHI, or other sensitive clinical information.

Report: blank/distorted images, missing or changed annotations, failed exports, login errors, or unusual delays. Report the image name and approximate time; do not send sensitive image data outside the approved support process.

Before reporting a display problem, try one normal refresh — but never refresh while a recent annotation change may still be saving.
